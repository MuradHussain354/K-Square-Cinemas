NIRAM

A Film by Krishna Balaram. Inspired by True Incidents.

NIRAM is an intriguing cinematic drama that explores the complexities of human relationships, love, greed, betrayal and hidden identities.

Set against interconnected lives and unexpected circumstances, the film takes audiences through a world where appearances can be deceptive and every character carries a secret.

With an engaging narrative and unexpected turns, NIRAM promises an emotionally layered cinematic experience.

Film synopsis

Every Character Has a Secret. Every Secret Has a Consequence.

A former army recruit turned social-media entrepreneur crosses paths with a woman who refuses love but lives only for her mother. At the same time, a wealthy businessman falls for a mysterious woman whose past is deeply tied to betrayal, money and hidden identities.

As love stories unfold in different worlds, a sudden crime, a psychological breakdown and a silent disappearance trigger a chain of events spanning Chennai, Dehradun and Auli.

What begins as romance slowly transforms into a tense emotional thriller where faces look the same, intentions do not, and every character hides a truth, even from themselves.

The film explores love without possession, greed without limits and secrets that must never surface, leading to a powerful climax where life moves on… even when the truth does not.
